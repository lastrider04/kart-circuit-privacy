Kart Circuit – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Kart Circuit is an offline game. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • Game progress, settings and high scores are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • The app requests only the vibration permission (for haptic feedback). It does not request network, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.
